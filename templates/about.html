About This System

Smart Medical Cabinet - a prototype system for verifying medicines by barcode before dispensing, and keeping track of stock.

Dashboard

Shows a overview: how many medicines are in the system, which ones are running low on stock, which batches are expiring within 30 days, and the most recent stock changes.

Medicines page

Lists every medicine in the system. From here you can:

  • Add Medicine - register a new medicine and its first batch (expiry + quantity).
  • Edit - update a medicine's details, or add a new batch to restock it.
  • Import CSV - upload a spreadsheet to add many medicines at once.
  • Delete - remove a medicine and its batches.

Verify page

This is where staff verify a medicine before giving it to a patient. There are two modes:

  • Guided Verification - search for a medicine, tick the item and dose needed, use Locate to light up the compartment (simulated for now), then scan the barcode to confirm it matches what you selected. Scanned items collect in a list at the bottom; press Submit to save everything to the database, or Clear All to start over without saving.

  • Quick Scan - for when a barcode scanner is plugged directly into the computer. Just scan each item as you pick it up; it is looked up and added to a list automatically. Scanning the same item twice adds up to quantity 2. Review the list, then press Submit to update the database, or Clear All to discard the scans.
